This notice does not change its copyright holder or grant additional rights over
third-party code, measurement exports, datasets or publication materials.

DepMap, cBioPortal/TCGA/CPTAC/AC-ICAM, GSE178341, spatial source datasets and other
upstream resources retain their original attribution and applicable terms.
The archived cell-line matrix lacks its original release identifier and its
source-specific redistribution terms were not recovered. This remains a source
provenance limitation, not an implied open-data licence.

The code/data-only release excludes the manuscript, rendered figures and article
supplementary workbooks. Public study/sample identifiers retained in measurement
inputs support matching and auditability; inclusion does not change the sources'
terms. Source references and available checksums are recorded in `provenance/`
and the individual analysis modules. The repository maintainer remains responsible
for confirming applicable redistribution permissions before public release.
